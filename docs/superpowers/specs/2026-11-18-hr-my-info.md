# HR My Info additions
User authorized implementation of profile editing, PDF/Word uploads under Docs and Policies, Employment Details / Leave Policy and Guide text areas, and custom equipment. Clarified: both staff and Admin can upload and add equipment.

Interpretation: staff can upload supporting files to their own employee record and add self-reported equipment for themselves. Active internal Admins can upload and manage employee_shared personnel files without an employee profile or a module grant. hr_private, medical, compensation and case documents retain existing class-specific grant gates. Official policy publication and employment detail editing retain existing permissions. No module permission grants. Active Admins may manage employee_shared files and equipment, as explicitly requested; restricted document classes and official policy publication retain existing gates. No email/login/role changes. Reuse existing versioned document, policy and custody services. Official policy files remain published by policies.publish holders; employee uploads do not publish company policy.

My Info is a shared personal navigation strip across Profile, own Documents, published Policies & Guide, and own Equipment. Manager views remain separate; personal pages must not leak other employees' files or equipment, even for HR managers. Other employee profile viewing does not show links pretending to be that employee.

Profile: self edit display name, phone, emergency contact, avatar using existing account/photo paths. Employment details are a per-employee HR-managed multiline field with existing employee version guard and audit. Owner can read; unrelated viewer cannot read confidential details unless profile.manage.

Uploads: PDF, DOC and DOCX alongside existing PNG/JPEG, maximum 10 MiB, actual content/type/extension checks, no executable or macro DOCX accepted. Private HR disk, authenticated attachment download, no public URLs. Staff-created files use own immutable owner/classification; cannot replace HR uploads, expose restricted classifications or operate on another employee. Reuse document version/audit machinery. Policy attachments belong to exact immutable published version; authorized HR can draft text and upload supporting file. Keep text-only policy publishing compatible. Company Leave Policy and Guide use existing policy title/body textarea with clear labels/shortcut.

Equipment: authorized HR can issue existing company assets; any active employee may add a custom equipment description to their own record. Staff additions marked self_reported; generated unique reference prevents claiming/reassigning existing HR-issued assets. Staff may mark only their own self-reported item returned; existing HR custody permissions remain. No automatic financial records.

Verification: owner/other employee/Admin/preview scopes; spoofed and oversize files; PDF/DOCX/legacy DOC fixtures; policy immutable attachment versions and stale publishes; HR employment content version conflicts; personal equipment isolation and tag collision protection; My Info UI actions/labels; local synthetic browser proof then normal merge/deploy/live read-only checks.
