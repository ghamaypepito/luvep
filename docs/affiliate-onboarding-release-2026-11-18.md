# EP affiliate applications and partner management

Applicants start at `/affiliate/apply`. Enter your contact details, consent to contact and confirm your email using the private link in your inbox. Use **Already applied? Request a fresh link** on the same page to renew access. The confirmation screen is the same for every email address. Submitting again preserves the original application details.

Admins and Super Admins open **Affiliate Partners → Applications** to review verified applicants. Approval creates an inactive partner profile. If an existing or archived user already has that email, approval stops for staff review. Declining closes the application.

On the partner profile, generate the agreement with the agreed terms, then choose **Send for signature**. Check the delivery result on the agreement. The applicant receives a private link for that version. Either party can sign first. If EP signs first, the document fields lock, so enter any agreed bank and contact details before the company signs. To change locked terms, generate and send another version.

The second signature on the selected version opens the account. The applicant can download the completed PDF and use **Set up your portal password** to start EP’s existing password reset flow. Private links expire after two days. Opening a link keeps its original expiry. Signing links apply to one application and agreement version. Generating a replacement invalidates the previous signing invitation.

Use the **Account status** filter on the overview to see Active, Awaiting signatures, Disabled or Archived partners and their referral statistics. **Disable account** stops portal access and referral submissions. **Archive partner** removes the account from the current directory and keeps the profile, lead attribution and private agreement history. Restore returns the account to the directory with access closed. An administrator can then reactivate an eligible partner. Archiving a partner awaiting signatures closes the outstanding invitation. After restore, generate and send a fresh agreement for both parties to sign. Earlier versions and their signatures remain in agreement history.

Existing partner accounts keep their current access. Their agreements and referral history remain in the same workspace.
