# HR independent review and verification — 19 October 2026

Execution: Native implementation in this chat, as selected by the user. One independent whole-branch review covered base `2bc05a0` through `ca00e72`. It reported no Critical findings, eight Important findings and one reminder usability finding. The implementer reproduced the findings and made the following corrections; no second independent review is claimed.

| Finding | Resolution and regression evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Preview inherited the selected employee’s wider report scope | Preview is limited to that employee and intersected with the real Admin’s report access; altered employee IDs and legacy team attendance endpoints are denied. |
| Archived employees retained explicit HR grants | HR enrollment or schedule archiving revokes all HR abilities, including queued/report/file checks. Archiving the last active access administrator is blocked. |
| Overnight drafts could not be completed after midnight | An open overnight shift or a shift closed during the employee’s current local day remains editable; unrelated historical drafts remain locked. |
| Profile edits flattened per-day working hours | Existing daily hours are preserved; replacing them with default hours requires an explicit checkbox. |
| Minute task boundaries rejected real clock seconds | Inputs at a real interval boundary align to its exact recorded instant; second precision remains available and breaks cannot be bridged. |
| Same-day clock-in reopened a completed DTR | A closed workday requires a reviewed correction; a new local workday may start normally. The previous permissive test was changed to separate workdays to match the approved closed-shift contract. |
| Former HR case owner could reply after reassignment | Actor and case authorization are checked again after locking the current case. |
| DST rollover advanced UTC rather than local date | Calendar rollover occurs in the shift’s recorded timezone before conversion to UTC; NZ fallback and overnight minute boundaries are covered. |
| Dismissed reminder lacked an indicator and retained stale time | Countdown follows bounded server time, leaves a subtle reminder after dismissal and disappears after finish, clock-out or stale clock state. |
