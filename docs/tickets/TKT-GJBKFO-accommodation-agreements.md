## Implementation

New agreements snapshot workflow version 2. The builder uses property IDs, an explicit duration and unit, and advance weeks. `AgreementTerms` validates and calculates both preview and issued data on the server. Client totals, calculated dates, signature evidence and Exalt signature overrides are not trusted. End-of-month additions clamp to the target month's last valid day before subtracting one day. Days use the New Zealand calendar. Quoted rent is used for advance and partial-week rent; separately billed utilities are not silently added. Short-stay staff enter the agreed inclusive weekly amount. Coverage never exceeds the tenancy end. Bond and deposit remain editable and the total adds them to advance and partial-week rent in cents.

Notes are escaped, bounded plain text with preserved line breaks. Existing agreement clauses and appendices remain in place. The separately witnessed declaration is left unsigned/undated by the new digital agreement flow; its witness fields are not completed automatically. The confirmed signatory must resolve to exactly one active Accommodation/admin account named Alane Beryl Lozada with a saved private signature. No fallback to the generating user's signature is allowed. Name, user ID, signature and source recipient email are snapshotted on issue.

Each listed tenant signs individually using the existing bearer-link signing surface. Partial signatures are stored with name, timestamp, IP and user agent. Duplicate signatures are rejected under a database row lock. The final signature stores the signed PDF and links a tenant in one database transaction. A PDF/linking failure rolls back the final signature and removes the new PDF. Only then is the signed PDF copy queued to the snapshotted primary source email and the existing staff notification sent. Standard mail queue failures must be monitored and retried; a failed email does not undo a completed agreement. Staff can explicitly resend the signed copy from the generated-agreement list. Clients can download the signed PDF from the confirmation page.

One tenancy/household record is created for the primary applicant, not one occupancy record per co-signer. It is Pending payment, with no paid amounts inferred and no occupied-room or active rent-roll contribution. Additional listed tenants remain on the agreement. Explicit prior source-to-tenant relationships are reused without overwriting identity/status; names/emails are never used to merge existing people. Separate source records for one person still need staff reconciliation. Historical signed agreements are not automatically backfilled.

For onboarding, the pending record is linked to the source while onboarding remains Agreement signed. The existing payment-confirmed move-in action activates that same tenant rather than creating a duplicate. Standalone pre-tenancy tenants remain Pending payment until authorised staff complete their normal payment/move-in review. Tenant Documents links the signed agreements. Issued-property and signed-agreement deletion guards retain these records. Existing grants do not permit unrelated department users to read or issue accommodation agreements.

Legacy agreements retain their issued amounts, dates, signatory snapshots and existing signing behavior. The new tenant/email completion workflow applies only to newly issued version 2 agreements. Janille's TKT-HOKBXI is the separate purchase-invoice and supporting-receipt workflow, already introduced by PRs #69 and #83.

## Review fixes — 20 November 2026

Signing and payment-confirmed conversion now both lock and reload the source before choosing whether to create or reuse a tenant. Move-in retains the pending tenant's actual payment amounts and corrected contact details. A persisted-value version is checked under the tenant lock, so a stale open form cannot erase payments or contact corrections recorded by another staff member; invoice totals are never evidence of payment. Generic tenant edits cannot activate pending tenants or change their signed property, and resident lifecycle actions reject pending tenants. Standalone signed applicants have an explicit, authorised payment/move-in confirmation action. Onboarding-linked tenants must use their existing payment-confirmed conversion flow.

Transient agreements carry their inclusive weekly rent without adding separate utilities. Deletion locks and reloads the agreement and retains any partial signature, completed signature or tenant linkage; unsigned PDF removal follows a committed record deletion. A selected signer's recorded name must match the issued party, with case and whitespace normalization. Unrelated portal grants do not expose the agreement's bearer link through onboarding details.

New regressions cover stale route bindings, payment gates and occupancy, partial/final signature retention, inclusive rent, signer attribution, cross-role access and actual React move-in controls. Tests use synthetic people and fake mail only. Deployment and ticket closure do not sign client agreements, change production payments, or send email.
