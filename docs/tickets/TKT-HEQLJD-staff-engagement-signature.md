# TKT-HEQLJD - Staff upload of client-provided engagement signature

The public client signing page already supports an uploaded image, but staff lacked an Engagement-list action with independent notification control. Add a separate authorized staff action; public signing behavior and its existing notifications remain untouched.

Staff upload accepts PNG/JPG up to2MB/4000x4000, requires the client signer name and explicit confirmation that the client provided and authorized this signature for the selected issued agreement. It only accepts signable Engagement documents in accessible Immigration cases, rejects drafts/rejected/already-signed documents, and requires the issued payload snapshot. Legacy agreements without snapshots require manual review rather than re-rendering changed live terms.

Reuse the agreement generator with its issued payload to preserve fees/names/adviser details. Store a new signed PDF and private signature image, retaining original PDF bytes/path/hash. Record staff actor/time/IP, signer name, authorization, signature hash and choices/results. List and audit trail identify Uploaded by staff. A shared case cache lock plus row locks guards signing/retry against the client signing path; repeat upload returns409 without notifications. Stage advances only from existing pre-signing stages for Written Agreement, with generic stage automation suppressed and no downgrade of lodged/approved cases.

Each audience has a mutually exclusive send/do-not-send pair; client and internal choices remain independent. Defaults send neither. Dedicated client email is optional; internal notifications use the portal database/bell channel and only explicitly configured active Immigration/admin users. No generic signed/follow-up automations are fired by this path. Notification errors are returned after the signature is saved, not rolled back or retried by uploading again.
